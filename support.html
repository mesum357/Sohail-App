Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Download the UBUX app from the App Store or Google Play, tap Sign Up, and follow the prompts to register with your email address or mobile number. You'll receive a verification code to complete setup.

I am a business and want to register with UBUX?

Visit our For Business page to learn how UBUX works for merchants, then contact us at info@ubux.app to get started. Our team will guide you through onboarding at no cost.

As a seller, do I need any other system?

No additional loyalty system is required. UBUX integrates with your existing point-of-sale workflow — customers simply scan a QR code or provide their account details at checkout to earn or redeem rewards.

How do I earn rewards?

Earn rewards automatically when you make eligible purchases at participating businesses. Present your UBUX account at checkout or scan the merchant's QR code — points are credited to your account shortly after your transaction.

How do I redeem rewards?

Open the UBUX app, view your available rewards balance, and select Redeem at a participating business. Show your redemption code or scan at checkout to apply your reward to your purchase.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password and enter the email address linked to your account. We'll send you a secure link to reset your password. If you need further help, email support@ubux.app.

How do I update my account details?

Go to Profile in the UBUX app to update your name, email, phone number, and notification preferences. Changes to your email address may require re-verification for security purposes.

I need additional help.

If your question isn't answered here, reach out to our team at support@ubux.app or visit our Contact page for other enquiry types. We aim to respond within two business days.
